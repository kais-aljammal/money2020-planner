The Playbook

Phase 1

The Hook (15–20s)

Establish relevance and explain the benefit immediately.

"Hi, I'm Kais. We built Interviewlary—it gives candidates realistic mock interviews for specific roles, with instant feedback on their answers. Are you involved in hiring or coaching?"
Phase 2

The Demo (45–60s)

Show the product's most convincing result. Do not run a full 10-question interview.

  • Target Role: Show the clean interface.
  • Question: Show a relevant job question.
  • Answer: Show the candidate's response.
  • Feedback: Highlight the actionable AI feedback.
Phase 3

The Close (2–3m)

Discuss differentiation: role customization, structured conditions, and saved progress. Acknowledge that it supports prep, but doesn't predict hiring outcomes.

Crucial Handoff

Agree on ONE clear next step (e.g., complete a first interview, arrange a trial discussion, or get a V1 update). A contact without an action is dead.
