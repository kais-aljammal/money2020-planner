The Flow
Step 1: The Hook (15-20s)

"Hi, I'm Kais. We built Interviewlary—it gives candidates realistic mock interviews for specific roles, with instant feedback on their answers. Are you involved in hiring or coaching at your company?"

Step 2: The Demo (45-60s)

1. Target Role: Show the interface.
2. Question: Play a relevant question.
3. Answer: Show candidate's response.
4. Feedback: Show specific, actionable AI feedback.
*Do not run a full 10-question interview here. Use fallback example if no internet.

Step 3: The Close (2-3m)

Discuss differentiation: role customization, structured conditions, saved progress.
Action: Agree on ONE next step (e.g. they do a trial, intro to HR, or agree to receive V1 update).

Roles & Responsibilities

Kais: Lead introductions, explain the problem, qualify interest, and close with a next step.

Omar: Operate the demonstration, handle deeper product questions, and record follow-up details.
